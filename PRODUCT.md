# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Frontend developers, engineering leads, and agent-tool users evaluating or
installing focused frontend skills. They usually arrive from a repository,
marketplace, or shared link and decide quickly whether the pack is credible,
relevant, and worth adding to their coding-agent workflow.

## Product Purpose

Explain why Frontend Niche Skills is different from a broad checklist, let a
visitor experience its symptom-to-evidence routing model, and move a convinced
visitor to a correct installation command. Success means a first-time visitor
can understand the value in seconds, find a personally relevant example, verify
the quality claims without marketing overreach, and install without reading the
entire repository.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and assured. The page should feel technically sophisticated
and visually memorable without using developer-tool theater, fake certainty, or
decorative complexity that competes with comprehension.

## Anti-references

- Generic four-square or abstract-network logos that could belong to any AI app.
- Dark terminal dashboards, command-center panels, or monospace-heavy developer
  cosplay.
- Long documentation walls, statistic-first SaaS layouts, and repeated card
  grids.
- Warm editorial paper, oversized display-serif headlines, and broadsheet
  styling unrelated to the product.
- Neon 3D imagery or glass panels used as decoration without explaining the
  evidence-routing model.
- Claims of accuracy, uniqueness, or completeness that exceed the repository's
  recorded evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Demonstrate the routing model before explaining the catalog.
2. Give each viewport one dominant decision instead of presenting the whole
   repository at once.
3. Make sophistication come from composition, motion, and interaction—not from
   clutter.
4. Reveal detailed proof only when the visitor asks for it, while keeping every
   claim traceable.
5. Treat installation as the completion of the story, not a detached code block.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, semantic
landmarks, and motion alternatives. The primary story and installation path
must remain understandable without JavaScript. Interactive visuals must not
depend on color alone, and reduced-motion users receive an equivalent static
state.
